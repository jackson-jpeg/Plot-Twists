**One thing to notice before you read them.** Nobody told the model to name the characters.
It derived a speaker name from each trait on its own — Chewer, Dreamer, Prophet, Echo,
Verbose, Secretary. That is the grammar doing what it was supposed to do: a trait is a
performable handle, so the model can build a voice and a name out of it without a franchise
to lean on. It could not have done that with "a grumpy swamp ogre" — it would just have
written the ogre.

## Deliberate public domain — a judgement call to confirm or overrule

You said genre and public domain are allowed and under-used. They are now used, and only in
