## 2. 🔴 The seat cap — the finding that gates everything else

### The game cannot seat eight. Confirmed two ways (rule 2.2).

**Mechanism 1 — reading the code.** `MAX_PLAYERS.ENSEMBLE = 6` (`server/utils/constants.ts:11`),
enforced at `room.handler.ts:186`. Not overridden anywhere: the only readers are two listing
filters in `matchmaking.service.ts`, the join handler, and one display field in `routes/api.ts`.
There is no env knob. The 7th joiner is **not rejected** — they are seated as `SPECTATOR`
(`room.handler.ts:203`). `game.helpers.ts:117-125` then builds the trait list from **PLAYER-role
selections only**, explicitly excluding spectators, so six is also the ceiling on what reaches the
model.

**Mechanism 2 — a live room.** Eight real socket clients driven through a complete ENSEMBLE round
against the real handlers, reading the prompt out of the mock Anthropic endpoint rather than out of
the source:

```
8 joiners → 6 PLAYER, 2 SPECTATOR, 0 rejected      (roster: 9 people, incl. host)
CRITICAL: You have 6 characters...                 (what the model actually received)
```

**So the room holds nine bodies; the scene holds six parts.** The host is role `HOST`, not
`PLAYER`, so they run the teleprompter without consuming a seat or contributing a trait.

## 3. Chunk 5 — the rename, complete

### The URL bug it uncovered — the real content of the commit

The rename was supposed to be mechanical. It was not, because the record's diagnosis of the
OG-image problem was **wrong in a way that hid a live bug**:

`next.config.js` declared `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL ||
'http://localhost:3000'` in its `env` block, which Next inlines at **build** time. So every
`BASE_URL || APP_URL || <domain>` chain in the app stopped at the **second** term. The dead domain
everyone was worried about was unreachable dead code.

What the live site was actually serving, read from the response rather than the source:

```
og:image  →  http://localhost:3000/opengraph-image
robots.txt →  Sitemap: http://localhost:3000/sitemap.xml
sitemap.xml → <loc>http://localhost:3000</loc>
```

**And because these are `NEXT_PUBLIC_*`, they bake into the bundle.** Setting the variable in
`/etc/plotslop/env` and restarting would have changed nothing. The recorded fix — *"one line plus a
restart"* — was wrong on both counts.

Replaced with `lib/siteUrl.ts`, one `NODE_ENV`-aware constant, and the `next.config.js` default
deleted. Verified against a real production build **with the variable still unset**:

```
<loc>https://plotslop.com</loc>
Sitemap: https://plotslop.com/sitemap.xml
og:image content="https://plotslop.com/opengraph-image?..."
<title>PlotSlop - AI Improv Party Game</title>
```
